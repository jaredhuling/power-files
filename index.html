Race reports and replays

This site indexes some cycling (road and gravel) races that I've done multiple times over several years. Each year, a given race plays out very differently. Here we explore various metrics based on power and heart rate data to understand how the race efforts differed from year to year. A key difference is often how the climbs in races play out, so I've created a 3d visual replay of how every climb shared between two editions of a race compared from year to year. The 3d visualizer shows power data in real-time and marks along the climb key moments such as hard surges and when minute best efforts are achieved.
